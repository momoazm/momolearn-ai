# IELTS Practice Trainer — 100% Free, Offline

A complete IELTS Academic exam simulator in a **single HTML file**. No accounts,
no subscriptions, no internet required.

## How to run

Double-click `index.html` (opens in Edge/Chrome). That's it.
Optional: right-click → Open with → Google Chrome / Microsoft Edge.

> Tip: use **Microsoft Edge or Chrome** for the best listening voices and
> microphone support. Everything stays on your computer — recordings are never uploaded.

## What's inside

| Paper | Details | Auto-scored? |
|---|---|---|
| Listening | 4 parts · 40 Q · 30 min · audio read aloud by browser TTS | Yes |
| Reading | 3 academic passages · 40 Q (TFNG, headings, MC, gap-fill) · 60 min | Yes |
| Writing | Task 1 with a real SVG chart + Task 2 essay · 60 min · word counters | Self-assessed |
| Speaking | Parts 1–3 · 3 rotating topic sets · mic recording + playback · cue cards · 1-min prep timer | Self-assessed |

- **Full Exam mode**: all four papers back-to-back with official timings and breaks.
- **Practice mode**: drill any single paper.
- **Results History** saved locally; export to JSON anytime.
- Raw scores convert to band estimates using the official conversion tables.
- Built-in **4-week free study plan** and **exam guide** tabs.

## Honest limitations

- Listening audio is a synthesized voice, not real exam recording. Treat it as
  timing/format training; use free sites (British Council tests) for real audio.
- Writing/Speaking bands are self-assessed against official criteria — compare
  yourself with model answers on ieltsliz.com, or get occasional free AI feedback
  (Write & Improve, SmallTalk2Me free tier).

## Free companion websites (see Study Plan tab in-app)
