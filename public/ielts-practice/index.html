How band scores work

  • Each skill 0–9. Overall = mean of the four skills, rounded to nearest half (0.25 rounds UP to .5; 0.75 rounds UP).
  • This app converts your raw L/R scores using official conversion tables and guides self-scoring for W/S using the real criteria.
  • Writing criteria: Task Achievement/Response · Coherence & Cohesion · Lexical Resource · Grammatical Range & Accuracy (25% each).
  • Speaking criteria: Fluency & Coherence · Lexical Resource · Grammatical Range & Accuracy · Pronunciation (25% each).

Tips that actually move bands

  • Listening: read questions ahead, predict word types (number/name/place). Watch for plural 's'. Spelling matters.
  • Reading: never leave blanks. TFNG: "Not Given" means the passage simply doesn't say. Don't use outside knowledge.
  • Writing: answer ALL parts of the prompt; clear overview in Task 1; 4-paragraph essays; count words.
  • Speaking: never give one-word answers — extend with reason + example. Fluency beats perfection. This app's examiner speaks questions aloud, cuts you off at real limits (≈40s in Part 1, exactly 2 min in Part 2, ≈70s in Part 3), asks a follow-up after your cue card, and prompts you if an answer is too short.
About the listening audio: this app reads scripts with your browser's built-in voices (works offline). It's a robot voice, not exam audio — treat it as timing/format practice. For real recorded audio, see the free links in the Study Plan.

📅 4-Week Plan — 100% Free

Week 1 — Understand the test + baseline

  • Daily 30 min: IELTS Liz (ieltsliz.com) — read lessons for one skill per day.
  • Take this app's full exam as your diagnostic. Record scores in History.
  • Start an error log notebook (why each mistake happened).
  • British Council free mock tests: takeielts.britishcouncil.org → practice tests.

Week 2 — Skill drills

  • Listening daily: BBC "6 Minute English" + this app's Listening (replay ON).
  • Reading daily: one passage/day from this app or engnovate.com (free tests).
  • Writing: study model answers on IELTS Liz; write 3 essays; score them free at Cambridge Write & Improve (writeandimprove.com).
  • Speaking daily: record yourself answering this app's Part 1 topics; listen back; note fillers.

Week 3 — Timed conditions + feedback loop

  • Alternate days: one timed section (replay OFF, strict timers) + review errors.
  • Free AI checks: SmallTalk2Me free tier (speaking), engnovate essay checker (writing).
  • Second full exam this weekend. Compare with Week 1 in History.
  • Vocabulary: 10 topic words/day (Anki free desktop app).

Week 4 — Peak + logistics

  • Two full mocks spaced 3 days apart. Review band descriptors (ielts.org).
  • Reread your error log — fix top 3 recurring mistakes only.
  • Day before: rest, skim models, prepare ID/logistics. No cramming.
All free resources mentioned: British Council practice tests · Road to IELTS free · IELTS Liz · IELTS Buddy · engnovate · IELTS Online Tests (ieltsonlinetests.com) · Write & Improve · BBC Learning English · Anki · TED Talks (Part 4-style lectures).
